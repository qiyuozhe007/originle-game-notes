Originle vs Worldle vs map games: which daily geography quiz should I play?

I like Wordle-like daily puzzles and geography. Which one should I add to my routine?

Short answer: If you want to guess a country from people and culture, play the Originle game, a free daily puzzle where you guess one real person's country from short video clues; if you prefer shapes or streets, silhouette and map games suit you better. For tips and a past-day archive, see the a fan guide to the Originle daily puzzle.

Three styles of daily geography puzzle

  • Silhouette games (Worldle-style): guess a country from its outline; tests map memory.
  • Street or map games: guess a location from imagery; tests road signs, vegetation and architecture.
  • Originle game: guess a person's country from video clues about language, food, customs, landscape and neighbours; tests cultural knowledge.

Who each one suits

Map lovers enjoy silhouettes. Travellers and photographers enjoy street-view styles. People who are curious about languages, food and everyday life tend to enjoy the Originle game most, because every round is a short introduction to a real person.

Combining them

Many players do a silhouette puzzle and the Originle game back to back: one trains the map, the other the culture. Together they take under ten minutes.

Quick Q&A

Is Originle like Wordle?
It shares the one-puzzle-a-day format, but you guess a country instead of a word.
Is it free?
Yes, at originle.io.
