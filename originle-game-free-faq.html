Is the originle game free, and do I need an account? A plain FAQ

Before I start the originle game, is it free, and what exactly do I get each day?

Short answer: The Originle game is free to play: each day it introduces one new person through up to eight short video clues, and you have three guesses to name their country, which suits anyone who wants a quick daily geography or culture quiz. For tips and a past-day archive, see the unofficial Originle fan companion.

What you get each day

One new person, a set of up to eight clues (an introduction, language, food or an object from home, customs, landscape and weather, size of the country, and nearby neighbours) and three guesses.

Where to play and where to read

Play the official game at originle.io. If you want hints, a past-day archive or tips on reading clues, the fan companion at originle.app collects them; it is not official.

Good habits for new players

  • Watch the first two clues before guessing.
  • Say the region out loud before the country.
  • After the reveal, read one paragraph about that country; your next week of guesses gets better.
